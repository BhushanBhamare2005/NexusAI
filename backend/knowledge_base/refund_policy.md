# Refund Policy
- Refunds are allowed only for transactions with status `failed`.
- Refunds are NOT allowed for `pending`, `success`, or `refunded` transactions.
- Only ONE refund is permitted per transaction. Duplicate refunds are blocked by TrustGuard.
- High-value refunds (> ₹10,000) require human approval.
- Refunds reflect in 3–5 business days.
